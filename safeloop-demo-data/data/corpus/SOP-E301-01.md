# SOP-E301-01: High condensate temperature or high V-201 pressure

## When to use

Use this procedure when TAH-301 alarms, when condensate temperature rises without a change in column load, or when V-201 pressure rises toward the PAH-201 alarm at 2.3 barg.

## Steps

### Step 1
Check E-301 cooling margin. Compare TIC-301 output with TV-301 position (TV301_POS). If TV-301 is above 80% open, E-301 has little spare cooling.

### Step 2
Check cooling-water outlet temperature TI301_CW_OUT. Normal is 32 to 38 degC. A rise with TV-301 wide open points to warm cooling-water supply. A difference of more than 5% between TIC-301 output and TV-301 position points to a sticking valve.

### Step 3
Send a field operator to E-301 to confirm cooling-water supply and that TV-301 moves.

### Step 4
If V-201 pressure keeps rising toward 2.3 barg, tell the shift supervisor. A throughput reduction is the supervisor's decision, not the board operator's alone.

### Step 5
Do not rely on PSV-201 (set 3.0 barg) for normal control. It is the last layer of protection.
