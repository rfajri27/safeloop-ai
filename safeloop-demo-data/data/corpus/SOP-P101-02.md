# SOP-P101-02: Switchover to standby reflux pump

## Purpose

Move reflux duty from P-101 to P-101B (or back) without losing reflux to C-200. Use it for planned changeovers and when P-101 shows abnormal vibration or bearing temperature.

## Steps

### Step 1
Agree the switchover with the control room. Confirm reflux flow FI201_REFLUX_FLOW is steady between 18 and 22 m3/h.

### Step 2
Confirm P-101B is available: no open work order, auto-start test current (see MAN-P101 section 4.1).

### Step 3
Check the S-101 strainer differential pressure on the local gauge. If it is above 0.5 bar, do not switch; tell the shift supervisor, because a blocked strainer starves both pumps.

### Step 4
Confirm the P-101B suction valve is open and the casing is vented.

### Step 5
Start P-101B from the DCS faceplate. Confirm discharge pressure within 30 seconds.

### Step 6
Open the P-101B discharge valve slowly while watching reflux flow FI201_REFLUX_FLOW.

### Step 7
Stop P-101 and close its discharge valve. Closing the valve prevents reverse flow if NRV-101 passes.

### Step 8
If P-101B does not reach rated flow within 2 minutes, restart P-101 if it is safe to do so and tell the shift supervisor.

### Step 9
Record the switchover and the reason in the shift log. If the reason was vibration or bearing temperature, raise a work order for P-101.
