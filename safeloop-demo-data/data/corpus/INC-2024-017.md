# INC-2024-017: P-101 drive-end bearing failure

## Summary

On 14 May 2024 P-101 tripped on high vibration. P-101B started automatically and reflux was restored after 40 seconds. No pressure alarm occurred.

## Timeline

- Over the nine days before the trip, drive-end vibration rose from 2.9 to 7.8 mm/s RMS, with a two-day fall in the middle.
- Bearing temperature rose 14 degC above its running baseline over the last three days.
- The night shift noted a noisy drive end two days before the trip; no work order was raised.

## Findings

Inspection found an outer-race defect on the drive-end bearing and water in the bearing oil. Water had entered through a damaged bearing housing seal.

## Root cause

Water ingress through the damaged housing seal degraded the lubrication and caused the outer-race defect.

## Actions

1. Replace the housing seal on both pumps.
2. Add a water check to the monthly oil sample.
3. Raise a work order whenever a shift log records abnormal pump noise.
4. Treat a temporary fall in vibration as a warning sign, not a recovery.
