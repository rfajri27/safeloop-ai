# INC-2023-009: P-101B failed to auto-start

## Summary

On 27 July 2023 P-101 tripped on a motor fault. P-101B did not start automatically. Reflux was lost for 6 minutes until the board operator started P-101B by hand. V-201 pressure reached 2.35 barg and PAH-201 alarmed. PSV-201 did not lift.

## Cause

Flow transmitter FT-201 was frozen at its last reading because its impulse line was blocked. The low-flow signal that triggers the auto-start never arrived.

## Lessons

1. The P-101B auto-start depends on FT-201 reading live.
2. The monthly auto-start test now includes a check that FT-201 responds to a real flow change.
3. A frozen transmitter can make a safeguard look healthy when it is not.
